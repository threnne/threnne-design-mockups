How it works

Three steps. Roughly ninety seconds of setup.

First time setting up an AO3 workskin? The middle step is a one-time copy-paste. After that, you're just writing.

  1. 01

    Write or import

    Type your chapter directly in the editor, or import a draft from Google Docs or Word. As you write, drop in workskins (text threads, social posts, letters, emails) and preview exactly how they'll look on AO3.

  2. 02

    Install your workskin on AO3

    On AO3, workskins are CSS files you save once to your account. Any work you post can pull from them. Our file includes every skin we offer, so this is a one-time step.

    In AO3: Dashboard › Skins › Work Skins › Create Work Skin. Paste the CSS, name it, save.

  3. 03

    Post your chapter

    Copy the HTML we generate. Paste it into AO3's HTML editor when you create or edit a chapter. Select your saved Work Skin from the dropdown at the bottom of the posting form. Publish.

What we do with your writing.

Nothing

Your drafts never leave your computer. Export is a copy-paste straight to AO3. We have no way to read what you're working on, because it never travels to us.

Everything you type is saved in your browser's local storage, a small space on your own device tied to the website you're visiting. Your drafts sit there on your machine and nowhere else. There's no server on our end receiving them, no database storing them, no copy anywhere but yours.

When you're ready to post, hitting export copies the HTML straight to your clipboard. From there, it's a paste into AO3. No upload step. No middleman. The handoff is between you and AO3.

We don't collect
  • analytics
  • accounts
  • logins
  • telemetry
  • email
  • uploads

One tradeoff worth knowing Because your drafts live in your browser, clearing your browser data or switching devices will wipe them. Export anything important so you have your own copy.

Looking ahead If there's interest, we'd love to build a downloadable version. Real files on your machine that you can save, back up, and reopen whenever, with no browser-cache risk. Still nothing sent to us. Vote on Canny and we'll build it if enough people want it.

WHERE WE ARE

Early beta.

Beta means the tool works, but it's not finished. We're still actively testing and improving it, which means you're getting it before all the rough edges are smoothed out. You'll hit bugs, some small, maybe some bigger. When you do, please tell us. Feedback from the people actually writing with this is the whole point.

Our Canny board is where we track everything: bugs, feature requests, progress, what's next. Submit ideas, upvote what matters most to you, and follow along as things ship.

Want to help?

Free, always. Built better together.

This tool is free and will always be free. No paywalls, no premium tier, no “pro features” hiding behind a subscription. But building it well takes more eyes than just ours.

Two ways to pitch in

01

QA with us

QA means testing things to make sure they actually work. Try out skins in real writing scenarios and tell us where they break, where they look weird, or where the output doesn’t render right on AO3. You don’t need a technical background. If you can write a chapter and tell us “this part looked off,” that’s the help we need.

02

QA something specific

If there’s a skin or feature you’d love for something you’re writing, reach out. We’ll build it with you and you can help us test it as it comes together.
