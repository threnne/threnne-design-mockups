How it works

01

Tell us about your manuscript. Genre, subgenre, word count, tropes, pacing notes. The more specific, the better the match.

02

We find your pair. Matched by genre depth, not just label. Both writers commit to a deadline and a feedback format.

03

Read. Respond. Repeat. Exchange happens through the platform. Feedback is structured — no vague "it was good"s.

04

Build your reader history. Each completed exchange earns you reputation and access to priority matching next round.

What we stand for

by the genre, for the genre.

Everything Threnne builds starts from a single constraint: it has to make sense to someone actively writing romance. No outsider aesthetics, no condescension, no pivoting toward literary fiction.

Reciprocal by design

The exchange is mutual because good critique communities always are. We don't build extraction tools. We build systems that require everyone to show up.

Open research

Romance Signal publishes its work as it's figured out. Datasets, methodology, findings — all public. If it's useful, it's yours.

No code required

AO3 Works does the technical work so writers don't have to. The tools should feel like extensions of the writing process, not obstacles to it.

Genre-serious

Romance is a craft. Threnne treats it that way — in the research, in the tooling, in every editorial decision about what we build next.
