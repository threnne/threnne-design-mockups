what we believe

Three principles, kept in public.

Romance Signal is opinionated on purpose. Here is how we handle bias, curation, and the communities whose signals we read. We document the calls we make.

01
We're writers building for writers.
Romance Signal exists because a writer wanted research she couldn't find. The engine, the watchlist, and the published work all come from that position.
02
Editorial curation is a known bias. We correct for it in public.
The watchlist is maintained by hand. We document why every entity was added and flag editorial picks separately from criteria-driven ones.
03
Community signals deserve careful handling.
The richest signals live in spaces that should not be commercially extracted. We measure manually where we should and collect nothing we wouldn't show the communities producing it.
