What this is

The research wing of Threnne. Built for romance, built for writers trying to understand what works.

Writers want to know what makes a romance succeed. We're working on the answer in public.

Romance Signal is the research wing of Threnne. Threnne builds tools and services for romance writers. Romance Signal is how Threnne learns what to build, by understanding the genre carefully enough to build for it.

The work has two halves. The first is the signals downstream gatekeepers already use, including Goodreads velocity, Amazon performance, NYT presence, BookTok trends, and news cycles. The second half is the signals the trad pipeline misses — community and platform activity before retail catches up.

Both halves answer the same question: What does a successful romance look like in the data? We put the inputs in front of you, in public, so you can do the work yourself.

What we believe

01

We're writers building for writers.

Romance Signal exists because a writer wanted research she couldn't find. The engine, the watchlist, and the published work all come from that position.

02

Editorial curation is a known bias. We correct for it in public.

The watchlist is maintained by hand. We document why every entity was added and flag editorial picks separately from criteria-driven ones.

03

Community signals deserve careful handling.

The richest signals live in spaces that should not be commercially extracted. We measure manually where we should and collect nothing we wouldn't show the communities producing it.
