what this is

what we believe

Three principles, kept in public.

Romance Signal is opinionated on purpose. Here is how we handle bias, curation, and the communities whose signals we read. We document the calls we make.

01
We're writers building for writers.
Romance Signal exists because a writer wanted research she couldn't find.
02
Editorial curation is a known bias. We correct for it in public.
The watchlist is maintained by hand, with every entry documented.
03
Community signals deserve careful handling.
We measure manually where we should and collect nothing we wouldn't show the source.
