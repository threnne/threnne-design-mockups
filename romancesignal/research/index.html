Volume 001 · May 2026 Latest

Off Campus and Heated Rivalry. What the Trad Pipeline Catches, What It Misses.

Two hockey romance adaptations dropped in the last six months. The industry caught one cleanly. The other was harder.

Chart 03 · The Pre-Show Asymmetry, Per Reader

Prime Video's Off Campus, an adaptation of Elle Kennedy's The Deal, premiered twelve days ago. HBO Max's Heated Rivalry, an adaptation of Rachel Reid's second Game Changers book, aired six months earlier. By every signal that gets weighted heavily, Heated Rivalry looked midlist right up until the show forced the issue.

Goodreads is the most important signal the romance industry reads. It caught The Deal cleanly. It did not catch Heated Rivalry the same way. The signal that was doing something unusual lived on AO3, in a small pairing tag that had been producing durable activity for years.

Whether that pattern is readable as a leading indicator, and whether it can be read ethically, is the open question this volume sits with.
